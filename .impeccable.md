## Design Context

### Users
University students studying collaboratively at night. They use Gommies during late-night study sessions to stay motivated, track progress, and feel socially connected with their circle of friends. Context: low-light environments, mobile-first, emotionally invested in streaks/XP/social dynamics.

### Brand Personality
Focused, nocturnal, alive. The brand words are: **terminal warmth latent**. The app is a productivity tool that feels like a social game — dense enough to communicate momentum, calm enough to focus in.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark neumorphic UI. Deep slate-indigo backgrounds (`#0c1322`), single teal-emerald accent (`#6bd8cb` / `#0D9488`). Surfaces extruded from the background using dual-shadow neumorphism. The mascot bear shifts emotional state. Everything glides — spring physics, staggered reveals, perpetual micro-loops on status indicators.

Anti-references: no neon outer glows, no purple/violet, no centered hero text, no identical card grids, no emoji anywhere.

### Design Principles
1. **Neumorphic depth over flat cards** — dual boxShadow system creates extrusion from the base layer
2. **Single accent, used sparingly** — Focus Teal (`#6bd8cb`) is the only accent; Alive Green (`#10B981`) is strictly for "live/active" signals
3. **Left-aligned, asymmetric layouts** — greeting, CTAs, section headers all left-aligned; hero row is deliberately 7:5 asymmetric
4. **No border-left accent stripes ever** — replace with full borders, background tints, or colored value text
5. **Spring physics, no bounce** — `bounciness: 0`, `speed: 100` for press; `tension: 40, friction: 7` for entrances
6. **Uppercase + tracked labels are section-level only** — never on body copy
7. **No emoji anywhere** — use Lucide icons or plain typography

### Font Stack
- Display/headings: `SpaceGrotesk_700Bold` (primary)
- Body: `Manrope_400Regular`
- Labels/metadata: `Inter_600SemiBold`
- Numbers/mono: `JetBrainsMono_700Bold`

- `surface`: `#141b2b` (Elevated Surface)
- `surfaceSecondary`: `#1e293b`
- `accent`: `#6bd8cb` (Focus Teal)
- `success`: `#0D9488` (Alive Green / active signals)
- `textSecondary`: `#879391` (Slate Mist)
- Shadow system: highlight `rgba(27,37,55,0.5)` / depth `#080c14`
